Privacy Policy

Amara Music — Discord Music & Community Bot by Team Amara.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, Amara Music may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, music activity, command usage, and server configuration data.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate bot features, maintain music queues and server settings, provide statistics and community features, troubleshoot errors, and improve the service.

3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in a database to support persistent features. Data is retained only as reasonably necessary for the operation of the service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Data may be processed by third-party providers that help operate hosting, databases, or music services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information. However, no online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data Requests

For questions about stored information or requests to access or delete data, contact the Amara Music team through our official support server. We may need relevant Discord IDs or other details to identify the data concerned.

7. Children's Privacy

Amara Music is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Users must follow Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Amara Music team through our official support server.
